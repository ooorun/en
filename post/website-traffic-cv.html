Useful Websites: View Website Traffic Data for Free via traffic.cv

2025-08-04 #Useful Websites#Free

As an internet surfing enthusiast, have you ever been curious about a website's traffic data? Perhaps you want to understand your competitors' performance, or maybe discover the next “viral” website.

Many people's first thought is the famous SimilarWeb, but its free version has many limitations: it requires login, and the visible data is limited.

Today, I'd like to recommend a newly launched website — traffic.cv!

What is traffic.cv?

In short, traffic.cv is a website analysis tool based on SimilarWeb data, but it solves several major pain points of SimilarWeb:

  • Free to Use: Check data for any website without fees, and with no quantity limits.
  • No Login Required: No registration or login required, ready to use out of the box, and no limitations on checking multiple websites.
  • Detailed Data: Provides rich and comprehensive website traffic data.

Hands-on: Scan a Website in 30 Seconds

To give you a more intuitive feel of its power, let's use the world's largest website, google.com, as an example to see what information we can retrieve.

From this report generated by traffic.cv, we can see at a glance:

  • Core Data: Google's monthly total visits reach 81.54 billion, users stay for an average of 10 minutes and 17 seconds, view 8.79 pages per visit, and the bounce rate is only 26.97%. These statistics directly reflect its massive scale and incredibly high user engagement.
  • Global Ranking: Undisputedly No. 1 in both global and country/region rankings.
  • Domain History: The domain was created in 1997, spanning nearly 28 years of history, making it a living fossil of the internet.
  • Traffic Sources: Over 87% of users visit by directly typing the URL, demonstrating its powerful brand effect. The remainder primarily comes from search and referrals.
  • Top Regions: Users in the United States contribute nearly one-fourth of the traffic, followed by Japan and India.
  • Top Keywords: The most searched keywords by users include gmail, google maps, etc., clearly displaying its core products.

More Than Just Traffic Queries

In addition to querying specific websites, traffic.cv offers several highly practical feature sections:

  • Top Websites by Visits: View rankings of top websites globally or in specific countries/regions.
  • New Website Discoveries: Discover recently registered and emerging websites to help you catch the next trend.
  • Fastest Growing Websites: Track websites with the fastest-growing traffic to understand the latest industry dynamics.
  • Keyword Traffic Analyzer: Analyze keywords and query keyword data for free (Direct Link).

Summary

Whether you are a marketer, product manager, investor, or just a curious netizen, traffic.cv is a rare and practical tool. It is free, efficient, and intuitive, helping you quickly understand the actual performance of any website.

If you find manually typing URLs too tedious, you can also install the free Chrome SEO extension AITDK. AITDK is created by the same team behind traffic.cv. Once installed, simply click the extension icon while visiting any website to quickly check its traffic data!
